Launch Plan — AI Document Chat (RAG)

One-page plan · v1 public launch · November 2026

Owner: Maya (Scrum Master)
Launch: Mon 16 Nov 2026
Status: Draft

Objective

Ship v1 of AI Document Chat to early users and convert the waitlist into active usage. Success = 500 signups, 50 weekly active users, and a sub-2s median answer latency within 30 days of launch.

Audience

  • Knowledge workers drowning in PDFs/contracts/reports
  • Researchers & students reading long papers
  • Small teams sharing document sets (legal, ops, finance)

Key message

"Chat with your documents in seconds. Upload a PDF, DOCX, TXT or Markdown file, ask a question, get a cited answer. No setup — runs in your browser."

Channels

Owned

Waitlist email (2 sends), in-app banner, blog post, docs site.

Earned

Product Hunt, Hacker News, LinkedIn, X/Twitter, creator outreach.

Community

Reddit (r/productivity, r/ChatGPT), Discord/Slack groups, university clubs.

Risks & mitigations

  • LLM cost overrun — default to extractive fallback; rate-limit per session; monitor spend daily.
  • Low retention — onboarding tour + sample docs; empty-state prompts.
  • Support load — FAQ, in-app help, office hours in week 1.
  • Privacy concerns — state "processed in memory, nothing persisted" prominently.

Dependencies

  • Landing page copy & assets (Marketing)
  • Domain + analytics (Eng / Ops)
  • Product Hunt listing assets (Marketing)
  • Support inbox & FAQ (Support)
